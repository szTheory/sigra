Round 4 refinement / ratified A1 rail-i / Space Grotesk Bold

A1, tightened up.

The gate ratified the A1 Rail-i direction and spent its one refinement round here. Two mandates: kill the ig-crop favicon (it read as Instagram) and push the wordmark's weak spots — tittle pop at 16px, g-tail deliberateness, tittle-vs-tail balance. Five candidates keep the Space Grotesk Bold rail-i spine and vary the favicon treatment, tittle geometry, and accent depth. Three favicon treatments survived the 16px kill test; the all-apricot favicon did not and was cut mid-round. Selection happens from this board — no further rounds.

  • Ratified A1 spine only
  • Instagram-free favicons
  • 16px kill test enforced
  • No background container
  • Subtitle-free primary
  • Ember hue 15–40° only
  • One refinement round

How to read this board

Every wordmark is the ratified A1 skeleton — judge the deltas. Favicon row first: D1/D2/D5 test the standalone rail-i, D3 tests an s monogram, D4 tests an abstract rail glyph. Then check the tittle treatment at the small lockup and whether the g tail reads as a deliberate rail.

Light lockups use ember-700 for small-size contrast (D5 keeps the ratified ember-300 as the control); dark lockups keep ember-300 throughout.
