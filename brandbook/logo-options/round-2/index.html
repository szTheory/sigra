Round 2 archive / compact marks / rail geometry

Sharper marks, fewer metaphors.

This board preserves expanded logo studies focused on compact silhouettes, rail geometry, favicon durability, and dark-surface legibility.

  • No crosses
  • No lock/shield cliches
  • No baked-in black wordmark
  • Favicon first
  • OSS tool, not SaaS fog

How to read this archive

Look for how each mark handles small sizes, color constraints, and the balance between architecture meaning and visual simplicity.

Wordmarks are composed in HTML so light and dark previews use the correct foreground color.
