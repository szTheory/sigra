# Disambiguate the duplicate login-page email label (v1.46 Phase 229 PROOF-03)

**Already diagnosed — do not re-investigate.** On the generated login page, once the
"Other ways to sign in" `<details>` disclosure is expanded, two email inputs render with the
identical visible label. The top-level magic-link form and `password_form/1` both label their
field the same way.

**The fix is decided:** the top-level magic-link form's field adopts the longer, already-in-file
wording `Email for sign-in link`. `password_form/1`'s field is left alone — under the
"or use a password" divider it reads correctly once its sibling is disambiguated.
