## Evidence Reconciliation and Lessons

The recurring trap was source identity drift between evidence snapshots: the secret setup note and Phase 248 verifier were captured before environment setup completed; Phase 249's verifier was captured before the final acceptance seal; and Phase 247 never received a standalone verifier. The fresh secret-name checks, preflight result, Phase 248 validation audit, and final acceptance are newer evidence, but they do not rewrite historical verification records automatically.

For future release phases, design a safe non-publishing hosted canary before implementation whenever success, failure, and cancellation depend on hosted event delivery or artifact retention. The canary must have no publish credentials, bind receipts to the source run, and let CI retrieve and validate every terminal outcome. Bind verification to immutable evidence identities and automatically mark reports stale when their inputs change. Keep secret values out of evidence; record only environment names, required secret names, policy result, source SHA, and capture time. These prevention rules are now recorded in `.planning/METHODOLOGY.md`.
