## SAFETY RULESET (CRITICAL — do not bypass)

1. **A zero-match grep must be a measurement, not a fatal status.** Under `set -eo pipefail` a
   command substitution whose pipeline starts with a non-matching grep aborts on the *success*
   path. Use a brace group with `|| true` inside the substitution; never a bare `grep | wc -l`.
2. **Every zero is paired with a positive control on the same surface** (project standing rule: a
   negative result needs a positive control). A zero with no control is not evidence.
3. **Evidence is captured at the final committed HEAD on a clean tree** (Standing Constraint 2).
4. **`mix ci`, never root `mix test`** (Standing Constraint 3). This plan does not need the full
   gate; plan 239-08 runs it. `MIX_ENV=test mix compile --warnings-as-errors` is the local check
   here.
5. **Found-while-cleaning becomes a todo, never an in-phase fix** (Standing Constraint 4).
6. **`scripts/ci/install-smoke.sh` leaves its app at `${TMP_APP_DIR}`** (default `/tmp/tmp_app`) and
   `rm -rf`s it on the next run. Set `TMP_APP_DIR` explicitly so the generated tree survives long
   enough to be grepped, and remove it when the task ends (REPO-01 was about stray artifacts).
