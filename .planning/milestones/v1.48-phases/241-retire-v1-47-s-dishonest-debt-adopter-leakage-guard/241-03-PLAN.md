### Settled: the p21/p10 delegation (research open question 1)

**Decision: `p21` implements leg 4 only and delegates legs 1-3 to `p10`, and the delegation is
asserted rather than merely commented.** Reasoning, recorded here so a future reader does not
"restore" the missing assertions: `p10` already implements id-resolution, step-parent and
display-name parity **including** the two edge cases a fresh implementation trips on
(`example_playwright_shard`'s `${{ matrix.seam }}` templated name, and the comment lines between
`install_smoke`'s job key and its `name:`). A re-implementation would red on day one on rot that is
not rot, and would create a second owner of one assertion — precisely the defect class this phase
retires. A prose-only delegation is not enough, because deleting `p10` would then silently reduce the
guard set from four legs to one; so `p21` additionally asserts that `p10` exists and still declares
its three test names. That converts the delegation from a comment into a contract.

   has it inside `example_playwright_shard`.

4. The doc lists `example_playwright_smoke` among the four app-behaviour lanes whose heavy steps are
   `docs_only`-step-gated; at HEAD that job is a pure one-step aggregator with no step gate at all.
   (Unnamed by D-15.)
