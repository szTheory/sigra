<objective>
Make `Sigra.Admin.Live.AuditIndexLive` the owner of every URL transition on the failing test's path
— its own preset, sort and clear anchors and its filter form — with zero change to rendered output.

Purpose: the captured RED from plan 236-01 names a product race in which the browser, not the
LiveView, owns URL transitions on `/admin/audit` — pressing Enter in a form with no `phx-submit`
calls `live_socket.unload()`, a one-way latch that destroys every view and disconnects the socket in
anticipation of a native navigation that is then raced against the join. This plan removes both
teardown paths: preset/sort/clear anchors become live patches, and the form submits through the
socket.

Output: six anchors converted, one form attribute added, one `handle_event/3` clause with a param
whitelist, and an ExUnit source-contract test that encodes all of it.
