<objective>
Bound every job's runtime explicitly, sized against real measurements rather than guesses, and make
the completeness property enforceable so a future job cannot land without one.

Today exactly one job in a 21-job workflow declares a timeout, and that one is mis-sized by about 16
times against its measured duration. Every other job can burn the 360-minute default. The sizing rule
is twice the observed duration with a five-minute floor, widened where the sample sits on a warm-cache
path, where the job is network-bound, or where the historical maximum materially exceeds the sample.

`example_playwright_smoke` is the hard boundary: it measured 28.5m and has hit 41.7m. A 30-minute
ceiling would time out the very runs this phase is judged on, so it is set generously at 45 and left
for Phase 235 to tighten once the post-reshape steady state is known.

Purpose: bounded failure instead of a six-hour hang, without breaking the measurement pair.
Output: 22 job timeouts and a committed per-job completeness contract.
</objective>
