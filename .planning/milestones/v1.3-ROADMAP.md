## Milestone summary

**Key decisions**

- Treat **999.1** / **999.2** as milestone-scoped hardening rather than unbounded backlog drift.
- Prefer **machine-verifiable evidence** (CI jobs, ExUnit, Playwright) for SEED-001 rows where human mail clients or live OAuth UX are residual.
- Keep **SEED-002** honest: v1.3 delivers assertions + one audited `Ecto.Multi` hot path; full hybrid → Multi conversion remains future work.

**Issues resolved**

- Planning validation debt inventory and waivers under Nyquist rules.
- Dependabot / Actions major lag on SHA-pinned first-party actions.
- Maintainer confusion from deprecated JSON `audit-open` documentation paths.

**Issues deferred**

- Full `log_safe/3` → `Ecto.Multi` audit coverage across all subsystems (**SEED-002** remainder).
- Residual SEED-001 items requiring real mail clients, live Google OAuth UX, clean-machine wall-clock, or backup-code **rotation** proof until product ships `mfa_regenerate_backup_codes`.

**Technical debt**

- OAuth audit assertions not claimed in v1.3 (AUD-03 scope boundary).
- `STATE.md` velocity tables for v1.3 were not maintained numerically — rely on phase `*-VERIFICATION.md` and this archive.
