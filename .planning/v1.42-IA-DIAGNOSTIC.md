# v1.42 IA Diagnostic — Advisory Persona-Panel Pass

## ADVISORY NOTICE

**This diagnostic is advisory only.** It is an early-warning and sequencing input — one
pass at diagnostic depth (headline findings), not the full Phase 209 evidence documents.

**The Phase 209 per-surface scored panel is the single binding gate.** Double-gating is
deliberately avoided (D-13): late-discovered issues surface in Phase 209 without requiring
a roadmap edit. A finding at diagnostic depth does not block any phase; it sequences
priority. A finding absent from this diagnostic but caught in Phase 209 is expected and
does not indicate a process failure.

**Diagnostic depth means:** One pass per (page × lens × question) cell. Headline verdict
(keep/tighten/kill) plus one concrete finding or a `NONE — searched for: <what>` token.
Full refutation prose and DOM-anchor evidence live in Phase 209 per-surface docs.
