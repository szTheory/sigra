<objective>
Close SC-1 by measurement: capture what PW-01 actually did on one real `pull_request` run and one real
`workflow_dispatch` run, and record the per-seam numbers the PW-02 shard axis will be sized from.

Purpose: D-31 makes this a hard ordering gate — PW-01 must be *measured* before PW-03 or PW-02 touch
anything, or the registration win and the sharding win become indistinguishable in the numbers and the
audit's ordering rationale is lost (Pitfall 8). This plan is the wave boundary that makes that
structural rather than advisory. It is also where RESEARCH assumption A4 closes: five of the six seams
have never been measured at step granularity, and a shard boundary chosen without them is a guess that
reproduces the TEST-02 "one leg idles" failure mode.
Output: `AFTER-PW01-PR` and `AFTER-PW01-NONPR` captured in `232-EVIDENCE.md`, plus an SC-1 verdict
section reconciling the result against D-13's stated expectation.
</objective>
