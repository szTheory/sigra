That would halt the window after six scenarios.
- The fake's verifier job concludes success when the matrix contains the six scenario names, but the hosted verifier job runs verify_sources on the decoded matrix. The 253-113 name defect would also have failed the hosted verifier job, and no gate modelled that.

Output: real-shape fakes, the response_shape gate block, the verifier-run job closed set and the real-shape verifier fixtures.
