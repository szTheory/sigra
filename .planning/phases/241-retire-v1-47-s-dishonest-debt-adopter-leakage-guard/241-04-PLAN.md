<objective>
Make the composite action visible to the action-pinning supply-chain guard in both directions, by
adding a separate composite-action universe and relaxing the inventory regex so a bare `uses:` is no
longer invisible — each proven by its own committed fixture.

Purpose: SC-4. The guard has never opened `action.yml` at all, and the criterion as written
("unpin one of its four") is **vacuously satisfiable**: two of the four are bare `uses:` lines that
the inventory regex cannot see even after the universe is widened. Closing only the universe half
would ship DEBT-04 "closed" with half the composite action's supply-chain surface still invisible.
Output: one widened contract test, two committed known-bad fixtures, one evidence file.

### Decisions implemented by this plan

- **D-18:** the requirement's diagnosis is wrong. `action_entry/4` returning `[]` for local `"./"`
  references is correct and deliberately blessed — a repository-local reference has no third-party
  ref to pin. The real gap is the two-entry release-workflow universe, so `action.yml` is never
  opened. This plan adds a **separate** composite-action universe and leaves the `"./"` head and its
  blessing test untouched.
- **D-19:** the second blind spot — the inventory regex requires a leading dash, and two of
  `action.yml`'s four `uses:` are bare. The regex is relaxed to make the dash optional and the
  relaxation is proven by its own fixture. Blast radius was measured, not assumed: 7 newly-matched
  lines across the widened universe, all pinned and comment-annotated, zero would-fail.
- **D-20:** the two-directional proof uses committed known-bad fixtures plus subject indirection.
  The real `action.yml` is never mutated and the working tree is never left holding an unpinned
  action.
- **D-21:** the rejected alternative is recorded — a transient `git stash`-and-run mutation of the
  real file, captured only as a transcript. Cheaper to write, but it leaves no committed artifact,
  so nothing re-proves the direction at the next HEAD.

### Settled: what DEBT-04 closing does and does not mean (research open questions 5 and 6)

Stated here so the next audit does not re-find these and file them as new:

- **The Dependabot half is out of scope.** The composite-action todo names two gaps: the pinning
  guard (this plan) and a missing `github-actions` Dependabot entry for
  `/.github/actions/example-playwright-boot`. Adding the latter requires editing

  D-NN authorizes that, and it is not named by DEBT-04 or SC-4. Task 3 annotates the todo as

- **`ci.yml`'s own action pins remain unguarded.** DEBT-04 and SC-4 name only the composite action.
  DEBT-04 closing does not mean the repo's action-pinning surface is fully covered, and the evidence
  file says so in those words.
