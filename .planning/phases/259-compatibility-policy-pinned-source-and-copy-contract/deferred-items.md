2. **Example host cannot complete a confirmation resend in either session state.** Anonymous visitors raise `BadMapError` at `test/example/lib/example_web/live/confirmation_live.ex:158` (`socket.assigns.current_scope.user` with `current_scope` nil); signed-in visitors are redirected to `/app` by the `redirect_if_user_is_authenticated` live session at `test/example/lib/example_web/router.ex:106` (both verified by running it). The generated host carries the same defect, tracked by `.planning/todos/pending/2026-09-15-generated-confirm-routes-unreachable-both-session-states.md`. Registry row: `confirmation-resend` (`not_proven`).
3. **Example login and MFA challenge pages do not render the flash in their own markup.** The followed `/users/log_in` page body contains no "Invalid email or password" text even though the flash map holds it (the echoed email prefill proves the flash reached the page), and the MFA challenge LiveView re-render holds no flash text. Not verified whether this is intended; it only matters here because it is why the parity rows compare the server-side flash map (and, for LiveView, the flash read from the LiveView process state) rather than only visible page text.
