<objective>
Consolidate the 6 trivial leaf guard jobs into one cheap `fast_checks` job
(CACHE-02) to cut per-job runner-startup overhead, while keeping
`release_ref_guard` standalone, preserving every guard's distinct failure signal,
porting the installer-audit PR-path gate faithfully, and rewiring `ci-gate.needs`
in lockstep — without touching any of the 5 protected required-check names.

Purpose: Remove ~6 runner cold-starts from every CI run while keeping equal-or-
greater quality signal on the gate; reconcile the stale CI required-check docs to
the live ruleset reality.

Output: An edited `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (one `fast_checks` job replacing 6
guard jobs, rewired `ci-gate`) and a corrected MAINTAINING.md required-check
section.
</objective>
