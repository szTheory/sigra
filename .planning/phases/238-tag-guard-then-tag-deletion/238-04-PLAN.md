allowlist, and a dry-run-by-default operator script whose destructive paths are exercised without
deleting anything.

never from a glob, because a two-component name and its three-component neighbour share a prefix

constructed and tested; 238-05 is where it is executed.

`scripts/maintainers/delete-planning-tags.sh`, with the script's dry-run path proven against the
