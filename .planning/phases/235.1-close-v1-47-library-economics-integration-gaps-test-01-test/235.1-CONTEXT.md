<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Consolidated library topology

- **D-01:** Preserve the current single `library_tests_shard` execution owner invoking
  `MIX_ENV=test mix ci`; do not restore the slower Phase 233 two-job matrix plus the
  dedicated scaffold receiver.
- **D-02:** Explicitly redefine TEST-02 around comparable, separately timed work classes
  within the one-owner parity run instead of retaining a literal requirement for two CI
  jobs. The evidence must keep library economics measurable rather than merely deleting
  the old claim. — **Reversibility:** costly — reverting this decision requires restoring
  the matrix, its aggregate dependencies, the partition consumer, and protected evidence.

### Same-run slow-test visibility

- **D-03:** Restore TEST-01 by producing deterministic slow-test timing from the same
  parallel ExUnit execution owned by `mix ci`. A serial `--slowest` run, a second full-suite
  invocation, or timing derived from a separate rerun is prohibited.
- **D-04:** Reuse `Sigra.CI.ExUnitTimingFormatter` unless implementation research finds an
  equivalent existing seam with stronger determinism. The CI owner must set the receipt
  path, preserve the artifact, and prove the formatter is a production consumer rather
  than a self-test-only module.

### Install and scaffold economics

- **D-05:** Keep ordinary tests and `ci.install_golden` in the contributor-facing `mix ci`
  alias and on every pull request. Do not demote upgrade, golden-diff, idempotency, or
  scaffold coverage to a non-PR lane.
- **D-06:** Add explicit same-run timing around the ordinary-test and install/scaffold
  portions and retain named exact-once coverage evidence. TEST-03 passes only when the
  measured receipt shows the subprocess-heavy class is not the dominant leg, or when a
  targeted remediation changes that measured result and a fresh receipt proves it.

### Traceability and terminal truth

- **D-07:** Update TEST-01/02/03 requirement wording/status only after the new deterministic
  contracts and same-run evidence pass. Record the TEST-02 reinterpretation explicitly;
  do not silently turn the old two-shard sentence green.
- **D-08:** Reconcile the already-verified DX-01/02/03/04/06 traceability rows from
  `Gaps Found` to `Complete`, and make the Phase 235 verification body agree with its
  passing frontmatter and 11/11 deterministic result.
- **D-09:** Preserve FAST-01's source-complete n=52/p50=469 attestation, its immutable miss
  history, and the protected 93-row GATE-05 ledger byte-for-byte unless a fail-closed
  ownership reconciliation is strictly required by the new timing artifact.

### the agent's Discretion

- Exact receipt schema, helper names, and step labels, following existing machine-readable
  Phase 233/235 evidence patterns.
- Whether work-class timing is implemented by a small Mix task, shell wrapper, or alias
  helper, provided `mix ci` remains the single contributor-facing command and timing cannot
  mask a failed leg.
- The quantitative comparability bound for TEST-02, provided it is justified from current
  same-run measurements and encoded in a deterministic contract rather than prose alone.

</decisions>

  proven by focused tests; it needs a real CI consumer again.
- `mix ci` / `ci.install_golden`: existing one-command parity boundary with ordinary and
  scaffold-heavy work already separated into measurable alias legs.
- Phase 235 offline attestation and metrics helpers: established patterns for signed or
  fail-closed machine-readable evidence.

### Established Patterns

- `Library tests` is a byte-stable required aggregate; direct execution owners may change,
  but skipped/green aggregates never substitute for observed work.
- Performance claims require real same-run measurements and retained failure outcomes.
- Coverage ownership is exact-set and mutation-tested; a timing refactor must not create a
  second suite owner or silently drop scaffold/golden modules.

### Integration Points

- The library owner in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` invokes `MIX_ENV=test mix ci` and feeds the
  `library_tests` aggregate, which feeds `ci-gate`.
- ExUnit formatter configuration and receipt upload attach to that same owner.
- Work-class timing attaches at the `mix ci` alias boundary and must preserve nonzero exit
  status from either ordinary or install/scaffold work.
- REQUIREMENTS, Phase 235 verification prose, and the milestone audit consume the final
  evidence only after contracts pass.
