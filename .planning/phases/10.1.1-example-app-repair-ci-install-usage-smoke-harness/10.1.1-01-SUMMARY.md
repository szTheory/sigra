Simplest fix would be deleting the key entirely (ex_doc default is already `["html", "markdown"]`). I chose to keep the explicit list with `"markdown"` added back because it documents intent at the call site and is resilient to future ex_doc default changes.

Operators running the teardown after a long UAT session are unlikely to scroll back to the top — duplication is cheap insurance.
