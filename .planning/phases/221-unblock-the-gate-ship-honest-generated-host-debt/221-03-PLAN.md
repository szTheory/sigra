<objective>
Pin the upgrade-smoke published floor to `1.3.0` by adding `SIGRA_UPGRADE_SMOKE_START_VERSION=1.3.0` to the `upgrade_smoke` job `env:` in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. This is the single sanctioned lever (Option 4a / D-13) that makes the smoke stop resolving to the stray `1.20.0` — research proved publish/retire alone cannot green the gate because `sort -V` orders `1.20.0 > 1.3.0` and `mix hex.retire` leaves `1.20.0` visible in `mix hex.info`.

Purpose: Greening PUB-01 requires the smoke's newest-published floor to be a `type=`-clean release. The pin is a minimal env line, NOT a gate-algorithm change (the durable retired-filter is Phase 222 / HARD-01, per D-13/D-15).
Output: One `env:` key on the `upgrade_smoke` job.

IMPORTANT sequencing (D-14): the pin target must be a PUBLISHED version. The override at `upgrade-smoke.sh:56-77` validates that the pinned version is published + in-series, so on push-to-`main` the pin only greens the gate AFTER v1.3.0 is live (Plan 04). This plan authors the pin; Plan 05 observes the terminal gate-green once the publishes + merge land.
</objective>
