<objective>
Add a top-level `:cookie_domain` option to Sigra.Config and thread it through the TWO cookie write sites (generated UserAuth remember_me, generated mfa_challenge_controller → Sigra.MFA.Trust), plus emit a boot-time Logger.warning in :prod when unset.

Open Q2 resolution (path (b)): `Sigra.Plug.FetchSession` is READ-ONLY for cookies. A grep of `lib/sigra/plug/fetch_session.ex` (2026-04-09) confirms zero `put_resp_cookie` calls — the plug only READS the remember-me cookie via `Plug.Conn.fetch_cookies` and `conn.cookies[cookie_name]` at lines 101-114. The `@default_cookie_opts` attribute at lines 38-42 is applied via `Keyword.merge` during `init/1` but is never passed to `put_resp_cookie` anywhere in the module. FetchSession is therefore EXCLUDED from cookie_domain threading. The two cookie WRITE sites are the generated templates only.

This is still a HIGH-RISK plan because it converts a compile-time module attribute (`@remember_me_options`) into a runtime-resolved function in the generated UserAuth template.

Purpose: Satisfy DX-04 — cookie_domain configurable with sensible defaults, all Sigra-managed cookies honor it, loud failure in prod when misconfigured.

Output: Single config key, two runtime-resolving call sites, one boot warning, two new test files.
